Each Month/{{ page.cum_label }} (Actual) cell draws two rings, each its own 100% Finished/Semis split, both starting Finished at the same top edge for easy comparison: outer ring = Saleable Steel, inner ring = Special Steel. Saleable Steel figures are Production data while Special Steel figures are Despatch data — different physical flows for the same plant, so the inner ring's proportions aren't a slice carved out of the outer ring's total. The Annual ABP Plan column uses target/plan figures for both, with no Finished/Semis split available for its Special Steel target, so its cell shows a single plain Finished/Semis ring only.
